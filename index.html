03 / Client case study

Home & Airbnb cleaningQuality Touch Cleaning

How Apex Media built the online presence for a Greater New Orleans cleaning company that has grown through recommendations since 2014.

  • Cleaning homes since 2014
  • Greater New Orleans
  • Homes & Airbnb rentals

The goal

Turn word of mouth into a presence people can find

Their business grew from client recommendations. When someone hears the name, or just needs a cleaner, they search, scroll and compare in a few minutes. The goal was to show up in that search and make a strong first impression.

What Apex built

  • A website

    Built qualitytouchclean.com: a page for each service (regular, deep, move-in and move-out, Airbnb turnovers), their story, the areas they serve, FAQs and a three-step free estimate request.

  • A promo video

    Created a video for the business, so new customers can see who they are.

  • Google presence

    Set up their Google account and made their business page visible online, so people searching for a cleaner can find them.

  • Facebook posts

    Manages the posts on their Facebook page.

What this means for your business

  • One partner for your website, video, social posts and Google presence.
  • Built local, by someone in the New Orleans area you can call directly.
  • More than marketing: custom software and AI automation when you're ready for it.
