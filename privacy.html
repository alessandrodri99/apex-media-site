Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information Apex Media ("we") collects through this website and how it is used.

What this website collects

This website does not use accounts, advertising cookies or tracking pixels. The contact form does not store or send anything by itself: it opens your own email app with your message filled in, and nothing reaches us unless you choose to send that email. If you call or text us using the links on this site, we receive your phone number and message the same way any call or text would.

How we use what you send us

We use the name, business, email, phone number and project details you send only to reply to you, prepare a proposal and provide the services you ask for. We do not sell your information. We do not share it except with service providers we use to run our business (for example, our email and phone providers), or when the law requires it.

Feedback and reviews

If you send us feedback about working with us, we will ask your permission before showing it on this website or elsewhere.

Services this site relies on

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which processes standard technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site. Links to other sites, such as client websites, are covered by those sites' own policies.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep messages only as long as needed to work with you and for our business records. You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you have sent us at any time.
